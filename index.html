About the project

1000 unread emails is a collection of poems made out of email subjects from big techs, social media, banks, streaming platforms and other digital services.

The project started as a workshop for MozFest 2026.

Anyone can contribute a poem through the submission form. You can also organize your own workshop using the facilitator's guide and the participant materials provided.

More notes on motivations here: eseldesvelo.substack.com
